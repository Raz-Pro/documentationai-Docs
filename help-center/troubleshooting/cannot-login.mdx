---
title: Cannot Log In to RazPro
description: Quick fix for login issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Unable to log in
- "Invalid credentials" message

## Causes

- Wrong email or password
- Account not verified

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your credentials">
    Make sure you’re entering the correct email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Go to `https://dashboard.razpro.com/forgot-password` to reset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Look for a verification email from RazPro and click the link inside.
  </Step>
</Steps>
