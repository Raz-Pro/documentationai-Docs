## Overview

Start using RazPro Business Solutions by creating your account.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://dashboard.razpro.com/signup`
2. Enter your email and choose a strong password
3. Verify your email with the code sent
4. Log in to the RazPro dashboard
5. Complete your business profile

You’re now ready to organize and manage your project documentation with RazPro!
