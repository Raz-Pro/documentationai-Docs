## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to a modern web browser like Chrome or Firefox
- Optional: Team member emails ready for invitations

<Callout kind="info">

This quickstart takes under 5 minutes. No credit card or downloads required.

</Callout>

## Create and Verify Your Account

Follow these steps to set up your RazPro Business Solutions account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">

  Visit `https://app.razpro.us/signup` and enter your email, name, and a strong password.

  Click **Create Account**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">

  Check your inbox for a verification email from RazPro.

  Click the **Verify** button in the email.

  <Image
    src="https://via.placeholder.com/600x300/1e5989/ffffff?text=Verify+Email+Screenshot"
    alt="Email verification screen"
    width="600"
    height="300"
  />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">

  Return to `https://app.razpro.us/login` and sign in with your verified credentials.

  You'll land on the dashboard.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Your First Documentation Space

Documentation spaces organize your business projects, QuickBooks solutions, and client guides.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Space" icon="folder-plus">

  From the dashboard, click **New Space**.

  Name it (e.g., "Q4 Profit Optimization") and select **Business Solutions** template.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Sidebar" icon="settings">

  Edit the sidebar: Add sections like "Profit Strategies", "Cost Reduction", and "Client Testimonials".

  Drag to reorder.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add and Organize Initial Pages

Populate your space with essential pages.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Manual Creation" icon="plus">

  <Steps>
    <Step title="New Page">

    In your space, click **New Page** > **Blank Page**.

    Title it "Profit Analysis" and add content.

    </Step>

    <Step title="Organize">

    Drag pages into sections: Nest "Reduce Costs" under "Strategies".

    Publish to make live.

    </Step>
  </Steps>

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Import Markdown" icon="upload">

````markdown
# Profit Goals

## Metrics
- Profit: `>20%`
- Cost Reduction: `<15%`

## Next Actions
1. Review QuickBooks data
2. Schedule advisory call
```
````

  Copy your MDX or Markdown file and import via **Import** button.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Invite Team Members

Share access with your business coach team or clients.

<CodeGroup tabs="Dashboard,API">
```javascript
// Dashboard invite (copy from UI)
POST https://api.example.com/v1/teams/{teamId}/invites
{
  "email": "coach@business.com",
  "role": "editor"
}
```

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/teams/{teamId}/invites \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"email":"coach@business.com","role":"editor"}'
```
</CodeGroup>

<ParamField path="teamId" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your documentation space ID (found in settings).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="role" param-type="string" required="false">
  Options: `viewer`, `editor`, `admin`. Defaults to `viewer`.
</ParamField>
